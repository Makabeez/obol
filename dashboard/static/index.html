The agent that spends.

Every team here is building a seller. Obol is the buyer — an autonomous agent with a USDC budget that discovers paid services, pays each per call on Arc, scores what it gets, and reallocates toward the ones that deliver. It learns who's worth paying, and cuts the rest.

What it's deciding

Selection is Thompson sampling on value-per-USDC: sample each provider's likely quality, divide by its price, buy from the best draw. Cheap-but-unproven services get probed; proven-cheap ones get exploited; a provider that confidently underdelivers — or is just overpriced — gets retired. No human in the loop.
